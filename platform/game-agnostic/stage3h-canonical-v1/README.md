## Direct answers

1. **Which canonical experts contribute?** Nexus plus its explicit inherited selector can propose native Forge actions. Oracle emits real temporal evidence, but its decision-value consumer is missing. Others are unbound, missing, or inapplicable in this task.
2. **Which interfaces were resolved?** Versioned Nexus/Oracle evidence packaging, observation/action identity and source binding, exact output preservation, explicit missing/abstention semantics and simulator-free matched experimental synthesis. Legacy engines were verified executable, not falsely mapped to Forge.
3. **Which reasoning deficiencies were demonstrated?** The inherited selector's unthreatened-target PROTECT choices concentrate catastrophic/harmful outcomes diagnostically. The existing blanket threat gate itself causes large hit loss and more catastrophes on fresh development panels. No general expert-calibration claim follows from structural confidence.
4. **Did Prime beat the best single expert?** No: zero paired DDV, regret or catastrophe improvement; choices are identical.
5. **Did complementarity produce value?** Not established and not testable here: only one qualified action binding, with no temporal action consumer.
6. **Did an expert harm quality?** Nexus has worse absolute regret/catastrophe than S0 despite higher paired hit. The distinct V2 candidate is worse than V1 on hit, normalized regret and catastrophe, and worse than S0 on both DDV endpoints.
7. **Did improvements survive valid baselines?** No accepted performance improvement. V2 fails both the inherited V1 and frozen S0 comparisons; Prime only matches V1.
8. **Diagnostic or established?** Adapter fidelity, deterministic replay and file preservation are software-verification results. All new competence, attribution and candidate performance findings are OPEN development diagnostics, not sealed scientific certification.
9. **What remains before valid sealed evaluation?** Complete a committed, scope-correct preregistration and unused sealed lineage; bind needed expert consumers/comparators, define abstention/risk treatment and perturbation/generalization populations. For a meaningful MoE synthesis claim, qualify another useful action contribution or a real temporal-to-action consumer. Stop at Stage 3H.1 development; no later stage or authority expansion is authorized.
