## Comparator audit and mathematical resolution

The same sets, including ties, are selected, so every deterministic uniform-set score has exact equalities R=N at H0 and R=B at H2. These are functional identities, not hypotheses of statistical equivalence. Abstention preserves both identities because N's fixed fallback is B. The audit and synthetic tests bind that source, not a new selector.
